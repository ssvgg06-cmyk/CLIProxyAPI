# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operators use this authenticated administration surface to inspect a Claude credential pool, trace gateway requests, and review account lifecycle and quota state. They work in a dense operational dashboard and need exact identifiers, fast search, and unambiguous failure states.

## Product Purpose

The pool provides a safe, persistent operational presentation of synthetic Claude account records while correlating them with sanitized New API request metadata. Success means an operator can paste a real request identifier, verify its source fields, and open the stable masked account associated with it without exposing credentials or private gateway data.

## Brand Personality

Technical, restrained, trustworthy. Copy should be direct and operational, with state and provenance clearer than decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style dashboards, decorative motion, invented metrics, ambiguous source attribution, full email addresses, exposed secrets, or visuals that imply a synthetic association is the actual upstream credential.

## Design Principles

- Preserve exact operational identifiers and show data provenance.
- Make search, source health, and account tracing the dominant workflow.
- Prefer familiar controls and dense, readable tables over decorative surfaces.
- Expose failure and stale-data states explicitly without fabricating replacements.
- Keep all credential and gateway-sensitive data masked or omitted.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, semantic status text in addition to color, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior.
